Complete Archive

Exports a complete backup containing all research paper metadata, all 10 rich literature review note sections (formatting, tables, LaTeX math), all uploaded original PDF documents, and all embedded note images/diagrams into a single portable ZIP archive.

Select a previously exported ResearchMate ZIP backup archive. The application will validate the archive structure, inspect the papers, notes, and PDF files, and allow you to safely merge or replace your library.
